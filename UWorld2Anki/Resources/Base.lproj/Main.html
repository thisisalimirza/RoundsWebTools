  1. Turn on the extension in Safari

    Safari keeps new extensions switched off until you allow them. When the settings window opens, tick UWorld2Anki.

    This quits the app — that's expected, and Safari needs it to pick up the extension.

  2. Install the AnkiConnect add-on

    This is what lets Safari open cards in Anki. In Anki, choose Tools → Add-ons → Get Add-ons… and paste this code:

    2055492159

    Restart Anki afterwards. There is nothing else to configure — no settings to edit and no permission prompt to accept.

  3. Keep Anki open while you study

    The extension talks to Anki on your own Mac, so Anki has to be running. Your questions and cards never leave your computer.

Using it

Open a finished UWorld test's results page — the table listing every question with its ID, subject and system. A Find in Anki button appears in the bottom corner showing how many you missed. Click it, and Anki opens with exactly those cards.

The toolbar button does the same, and also lists the question IDs and lets you change how they are matched to your cards.

If something doesn't work

No button on the UWorld page
Reload the tab. Check you're on the results table, not the dashboard or a single question.
“Anki refused the connection”
Anki isn't running, or AnkiConnect isn't installed yet — see step 2.
Anki opens but finds no cards
Your deck tags questions differently. Open the toolbar button and choose another search pattern.
